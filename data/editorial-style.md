# SkyLux editorial voice

## Core tone

Write like a serious sports desk covering a competition that is objectively far too important to eight friends. The humor should come from treating group-chat nonsense, fantasy excuses and old grudges with the language of press conferences, institutions, investigations, tactical analysis and breaking news.

The copy should feel confident, dry and specific rather than random or meme-heavy. Use real scores, waiver moves, benches, player hauls and H2H context first; jokes should attach to actual events.

## Article structure

Typical preview:
1. Short opening that frames the week-wide narrative.
2. Waiver/trade market recap when relevant.
3. One section per H2H matchup with actual fantasy context.
4. A prediction or editorial lean for each matchup.
5. Optional interview / press-conference section based on manager quotes.
6. Short closing rundown of the biggest storylines.

Typical recap:
1. Results block.
2. One section per matchup.
3. Best/worst fantasy decisions, bench points, standout players and luck swings.
4. Quotes from the group when available.
5. Closing state-of-the-league paragraph.

## Style rules

- Croatian/Bosnian/Herzegovinian conversational sports-column language is welcome; do not over-standardize the voice.
- Quotes supplied by managers may be reproduced when appropriate, but do not invent direct quotes.
- Use bold sparingly for punchlines, scores and key phrases.
- Prefer callbacks that are triggered by current data. Do not force every running joke into every article.
- Make losers funny without making the article cruel; the league is built on reciprocal friend-group roasting.
- Do not insult people based on nationality, ethnicity, religion or other protected traits. Regional/Livno rivalry can be treated as football/friend-group banter without identity-based degradation.
- Old disputed transfers or governance stories are 'league lore', 'archived controversy', 'old case files' or similar framing, not verified allegations.
